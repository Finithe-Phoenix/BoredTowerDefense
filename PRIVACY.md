# Privacy Policy for Bored Tower Defense

**Last Updated: February 8, 2026**

## Introduction

Bored Tower Defense ("we", "our", or "the game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our game.

## Information We Collect

### Locally Stored Data

Bored Tower Defense stores all game data **locally on your device only**. We do NOT send any data to external servers or third parties. The following information is stored using your device's local storage:

#### Game Progress
- Level completion status and unlocked levels
- High scores and star ratings for each level
- Best wave reached for each level
- Meta currency balance
- Upgrade levels for permanent improvements
- Tutorial completion status

#### Game Settings
- Music volume
- Sound effects volume
- Display options (FPS counter, grid display)
- Visual effects settings (particles, screen shake)
- Graphics quality preset
- Auto-quality toggle

#### Game Statistics
- Total score accumulated
- Total enemies defeated
- Total towers placed
- Total abilities used
- Total games played
- Total victories
- Total play time

## How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally and used solely for:

1. **Saving Your Progress**: Preserving your game progress between sessions
2. **Personalizing Experience**: Remembering your preferred settings and configurations
3. **Tracking Achievements**: Recording your statistics and accomplishments

**We do NOT:**
- Send your data to any servers
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising
- Track your behavior outside the game
- Collect any personally identifiable information (PII)

## Data Storage Location

### Web Version
- Data is stored in your browser's LocalStorage
- Data persists until you clear your browser's cache/storage
- Different browsers maintain separate storage

### Mobile Version (iOS/Android)
- Data is stored in the app's local storage directory
- Data persists until you uninstall the app
- Data is included in device backups (if enabled)

### Desktop Version
- Data is stored in your system's application data directory
- Data persists until you uninstall the app or manually delete it

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data never leaves your device
- Security depends on your device's security measures
- We recommend using device encryption and screen locks for additional protection

## Your Data Rights

You have complete control over your data:

### Export Data
The game includes functionality to export all your game data as a JSON backup file for your records.

### Delete Data
You can delete your data at any time through:
- **Clear Progress**: Removes level progress and statistics while keeping settings
- **Clear All**: Removes all game data including settings
- **Uninstalling**: Completely removes all game data from your device

### Data Portability
You can export and import your game data using the built-in backup/restore features.

## Third-Party Services

Bored Tower Defense does NOT currently integrate with:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media platforms
- Cloud storage services
- Online leaderboards or multiplayer features

If we add any third-party services in the future, we will update this policy and notify users.

## Children's Privacy

Bored Tower Defense does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. The game is designed to work entirely offline with local storage only.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Audio and Media

The game plays audio (music, sound effects, and ambient soundscapes) locally on your device. No audio is recorded or transmitted. Volume preferences are stored locally as described above.

## Permissions

### Web Version
- **Local Storage**: To save game progress and settings
- **Audio Playback**: To play game sounds and music

### Mobile Version
- **Storage**: To save game data locally
- **Audio**: To play game sounds and music

We do NOT request:
- Camera access
- Microphone access
- Location data
- Contacts
- Phone information
- Network access (except for initial download)

## Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device indefinitely until you choose to:
- Clear the data through in-game options
- Clear your browser storage (web version)
- Uninstall the app (mobile/desktop)
- Manually delete the application data
